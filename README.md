# sahflow
A personalised daily planner designed to help people organise activities, stay on track, and adapt when plans change.
About SahFlow

SahFlow is a personal project inspired by a real-life need: finding a more flexible and personalised way to manage everyday responsibilities.

The goal is to build a friendly daily planner that helps people organise their activities, remember important tasks, adapt when plans change, and track their progress without feeling overwhelmed by their schedules.

Our Vision

To help people manage their days without making them feel controlled by their schedules.

Planned Features

- Daily agenda organised by time
- Customisable activity reminders
- Activity postponement and rescheduling
- Recurring activities
- Categories and priorities
- Progress tracking
- Review of unfinished activities

Development Status

Currently in the planning and early development stage.
